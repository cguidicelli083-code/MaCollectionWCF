## Full description (4000 char max) — English
```
Ma Collection WCF helps you catalog your World Collectable Figure (Banpresto) collection: One Piece, Dragon Ball Z/Super, Naruto, Demon Slayer, Hunter x Hunter, Chainsaw Man, Marvel, Ken Shimura and more.

📷 QUICK ADD
Scan a barcode, take a photo (AI automatically recognizes the character), pick a photo from your gallery, or enter everything manually.

📚 BUILT-IN ENCYCLOPEDIA
Browse the figure catalog and add an entry straight to your collection or wishlist.

❤️ WISHLIST & 💎 TOTAL VALUE
Keep track of the figures you're after, and follow the estimated value of your whole collection.

📊 PRICE ESTIMATES
Automatic estimate based on comparable eBay listings (condition, box, accessories taken into account), with an AI-based fallback estimate when needed.

💾 BACKUP & EXPORT
Back up your entire collection (data + photos) to a file of your choice, restorable anytime. Also export as an Excel spreadsheet, or import an existing collection from an Excel/CSV file.

🌍 AVAILABLE IN 11 LANGUAGES
French, English, Spanish, Italian, German, Portuguese, Russian, Greek, Turkish, Japanese, Chinese.

🎨 CUSTOMIZABLE THEMES
Choose a visual theme per licence, and change the app's background image.

Ma Collection WCF is free and funded by unobtrusive ads (a banner + a single interstitial when viewing the total value).
```
